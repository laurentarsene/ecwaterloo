What is a social grocery?

It is a shop for people going through a difficult time. You choose your own products, fresh or basic, and you pay a reduced, solidarity price, just like in a normal shop.

For us, the grocery is above all a door. Many people come for their shopping, have a coffee, and then talk about the rest: a bill, a letter, a housing or health problem. Our volunteers take the time to listen, then help you move forward, one step at a time.

We can help you with

  • Food

    Access to the social grocery, fresh and seasonal products, hygiene products and food aid.

  • Work

    Help with your CV with our IT team, cover letters, online procedures, interview preparation, and referral to the right organisations or employers.

  • Housing

    We are not a housing agency, but we can help with some procedures and refer you to the right services.

  • Legal & paperwork

    Understanding a letter, preparing some procedures, finding the right people. We work with legal professionals.

  • Health

    We do not replace doctors: we listen, refer you and help you contact a doctor, a hospital, your health insurance (mutuelle) or a psychologist.

  • Budget

    We are not a debt mediation service, but we help you understand a letter or a bill, look for the support you may be entitled to, and refer you to specialised services.

You don't speak French well?

You are welcome all the same. If you can, come with someone who can translate, or write to us in your own language at infos.ecwaterloo@gmail.com: we will find a way together.

Students

On the first Thursday of each month, the grocery is open to students: you leave with your shopping for €5. Online registration is required.
